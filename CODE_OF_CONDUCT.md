# Code of Conduct

AmphiLens welcomes constructive participation from researchers, conservation
practitioners, engineers, annotators, and community contributors.

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and collaborative. Do
not harass, discriminate against, or threaten others; do not publish private
information; and do not submit datasets, credentials, or other sensitive
material without permission.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when conduct conflicts with these expectations. Report concerns
privately to the repository maintainer.
